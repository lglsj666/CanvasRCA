## SeeRepo: direct PDF review and transfer limits

Read the supplied [LLM Agents Can See Code Repositories, arXiv:2606.14061v4](../../../docs/LLM_Agents_Can_See_Code_Repositories_2606.14061v4.pdf), including method, setup, results, ablations and limitations. Ma et al., version 4 (2026-08-03), ASE 2026 as identified in the supplied paper; canonical [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14061), DOI 10.1145/3832783.3834363. This session inspected the paper, not a fresh implementation of its code.

- SeeRepo extracts contains/imports/invokes/inherits relations, queries bounded neighborhoods, and draws Graphviz structure alongside code. This is a multi-step software agent; CanvasRCA still performs one diagnosis call, with deterministic preprocessing only.
- GPT-5-mini pure visual resolves 41.4 versus 55.0 for its baseline and costs more; hybrid reaches 55.4 while saving about 26% total cost. GPT-5.1 hybrid saves cost but reduces resolution (48.8 versus 51.0). Thus it does not establish visual or hybrid dominance across models.
- Its tabular layout reaches 56.2, nested layout 55.8, graph layout 55.4; graph's cost tradeoff is useful, not proof arrows always maximize performance. A depth-four setting reaches 57.2, while the adaptive depth setting is cheaper. Avoid transferring the best point without its tradeoff.
- Localization-stage visualization works better than repair/validation-stage use in that system. Much of the saving comes from fewer exploration calls. That mechanism is unavailable to one-call RCA and is not counted as a promised saving here.
- Borrow the separation of exact content from inspectable relationships, bounded association effort and explicit source anchors. **Do not borrow model-driven retrieval or claim a pod join was invented by this experiment.** Our unchanged full source ledger and selective deployment transfer differ from a focal code-entity neighborhood query.

The pruning arm inspiration primarily comes from our own paired failure analysis, not an asserted paper theorem. Historical FEATHER/pruning literature entries remain context about localization-sensitive evaluation; no new reproduction or verified universal pruning prescription is claimed.

### Evidence-to-design mapping

1. Repeated pod/service/node tradeoffs and one-hop QA failures → explicitly typed pod-mediated joins, complete membership retention, and a deployment-only condition.
2. Parent-vs-child, reference-vs-current, and threshold-vs-population substitutions → a source-complete population phase-comparison component, not new anomaly scores or more selected pairs.
3. Improvements from either ingredient can be destroyed by their combination → retain marginal-only, deployment-only and composed conditions, plus a same-fact join-layout control. Never choose the winning condition by true fault type.
4. Old gains may come from redundant highlighting, while RQ4.5 achieves exclusivity → retain exclusive ownership and exact original source records; do not restore duplicate diagnostic text.
5. Wrong causal stories can survive correct readout → independently score reading and inspect reason/ranking consistency; do not promise layout alone solves reasoning.

All count tables are descriptive; existing reports' corrected inference remains authoritative. No new significance claim or new model call is made here.
