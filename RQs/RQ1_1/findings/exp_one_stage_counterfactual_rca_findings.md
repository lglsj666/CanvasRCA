# One-stage counterfactual RCA findings

Status: registered and implemented; qualification is intentionally deferred
until the active RQ2 formal experiment completes.

No model result or scientific finding exists yet. The experiment will compare
the factual RQ1.1 renderer-v14 dashboard with label-blind targeted identity
transplants, matched placebo transplants, and a neutral no-evidence image. Its
purpose is to test whether root-cause rankings move in the direction implied by
changed visual semantics. It does not reuse the RQ2 renderer or design space.

After RQ2 completion, this file must be updated only after the bounded smoke,
artifact verification, two-model formal run, and paired CVI/MRR analysis have
finished. A rank change without factual-image accuracy gain must be reported as
visual sensitivity, not as improved or faithful RCA.
