## Attention, integrity and handoff

Slots 2, 4 and 11 enable passive summaries at the first full-attention layer:
last prompt query and first decode query. Both text and image regions are
recorded. The asynchronous store retains at most 64 KiB per call; its existing
total bound including overhead is 730,464,256 bytes per 10,000 calls. No full
attention matrices, Q/K/V or per-call heatmap files are saved.

Native/identity logits are captured only at prefill and existing decode
positions 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32 during the same generation. Require identical greedy
tokens, max absolute logit error ≤0.125 and mean absolute error ≤0.01; report
relative error with denominator clamped at 0.001. Positions beyond EOS remain
untested. Neither a successful tiny-model test nor all generated slots alone
implies these predicates passed.

The two single-request pairs measure the combined identity-adapter plus
attention overhead against native; they cannot separately attribute those
overheads. The CVC call supplies the actual selection/copy/generation total.
These few timing observations do not establish zero overhead or full-queue
resource sufficiency. Final input/output review, attention/speed assessment and
formal resource estimate remain explicit post-smoke work. The supervisor can
only mark `completed_pending_review`, failure, interruption or timeout; it never
issues formal authorization.

Track `summary.json`, `status.json`, `worker.json` and `runner.log`. Creating
`STOP` terminates the owned worker group without launching a follow-on. The
supervisor preserves spent calls, responses and atomic completion markers.
There is no automatic retry or reset of the logical smoke clock.
