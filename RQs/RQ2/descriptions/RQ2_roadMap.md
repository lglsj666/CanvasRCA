# RQ2 roadmap

1. Keep the clean RQ2 lineage and paired RQ1.1 headline incidents.
2. Treat evidence selection and visual composition as separate auditable decisions.
3. Group compatible atomic facts into coordinated, human-readable evidence cards, then require an exact one-card-to-one-silhouette fact-preserving conversion before studying layout.
4. Keep the superseded atomic sparse layout only as a low-density control; it cannot define or win the default design search.
5. Use finite grid capacity, non-overlap, local clipping, and secondary packing-density objectives so the dashboard is readable without rewarding noise.
6. Separate logical card size, raster resolution, and relative legibility; analyze actual processor geometry rather than nominal PNG size alone.
7. Screen a constrained D-optimal panel on development, then independently confirm only pre-frozen decisive contrasts.
8. Use full-card designs to isolate encoding/coordination/packing effects; use equal-fact Canvas/Text twins, random selection, and blank/reflow controls to isolate content, transport, and reclaimed space at the RCA endpoint.
9. Treat density as two registered interventions: compare top-12 lane/heatmap/overlay encodings at fixed facts, then compare a top-24 Dense Canvas only with its equal-fact Dense Text twin. Interpret dense-versus-normal as a combined content-and-compression effect.
10. Select fixed design/content policies only from their authorized RQ2 role, then assess text/screenshot/canvas and skin/spatial robustness without changing actors or prompts.
11. Carry stable card, silhouette, placement, cost, and outcome anchors into RQ3 contribution-identifiability work.
12. Before later Composer training, require exact tool replay and state hashes. SFT teaches legal calls; RL may optimize selection/composition only after attribution checks are credible.
13. Restrict every conclusion to the models, datasets, design bounds, budgets, and integrity checks that actually pass.
14. Keep packed QA/perception code as abandoned audit material, but never schedule it or use it to select D*/C*, verify active runs, or support an RQ2 claim. Keep same-call RCA attention as a correlational diagnostic.
15. Keep one RQ2-local minimal adaptation of the selected SIRCL* RCA prompt fixed across all designs and content policies; permit only the registered variable-card/design-control wording, keep the analyzer and verify/revise method aligned with its parent, and give dashboard decoding grammar only to arms that actually contain a dashboard.
16. In locked transfer, use compact equal-fact text at FULL, C*, and dense content to distinguish serialization compression from spatial visual design. Treat latency, throughput, VRAM, KV-cache, GPU-seconds, energy, and renderer time as hardware-specific operational diagnostics rather than efficacy.
17. Cross a small set of deterministic, label-blind RCA evidence selectors with exact-fact Text/Canvas twins. Interpret within-tool Canvas−Text as representation and between-tool differences as evidence-selection effects; do not describe the selectors as interactive agents or exact reproductions of upstream systems.
18. Reuse the complete frozen RQ1.1 480-case manifest after V3 regeneration. Keep AegisLab/AIOPS as headline strata and RE2-OB/RE2-TT as saturated-reference/OOD strata.
19. Enforce one aggregate ceiling of 40,000 model calls across all RQ2 subexperiments. A new subexperiment must fit inside the remaining RQ2 budget rather than receiving a fresh 40,000-call allowance.
