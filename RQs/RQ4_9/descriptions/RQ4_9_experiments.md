# Registration and interpretation

The authoritative complete protocol is `docs/experiment_plans/RQ4_9_Dashboard_Organization_Compression_and_Attention_Plan_2026-10-21.md`; machine-readable registration is `configs/protocol_v1.json`. Its 23 conditions include U28. U28 is a new finite source selection under a 4096-square, 28-panel limit; it is not a rerun of historical U.

The five questions concern organization, fusion, pruning, attention redistribution and quantity/readability. Comparisons use the five independently registered Holm families. Means and event-group signed ranks answer different questions and are reported separately. All cases are exposed development data. No novelty claim is attached to cropping, mean pooling, modality mixing or local labels themselves.

Historical-source adapters are hash-bound and read-only. Pair samples, population summaries and conditional statistics remain distinct. Hybrid source records and all their derivatives move together. Zero eligible pairs is an explicit no-intervention case; it cannot be used to hide rendering failures.

The code does not change Codex.md. Hybrid authorization comes from the user's RQ4.9 request, not a silent project-wide policy change.
