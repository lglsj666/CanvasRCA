## DD-RQ45-14: Qualify a native BF16 vision-attention successor

**Date:** 2026-10-19. **Status:** provisional; actual encoder and complete smoke pending.

**Context.** The measured H100 path spends approximately 117 seconds in a large
image encoder. Primitive job 24586861 reproduced this: FA3 takes 4.319 seconds
per attention block at 65160 patches, versus 0.0794 seconds for PyTorch Flash
SDPA on identical BF16 Q/K/V. All three tested lengths pass the predeclared
numerical tolerance. These measurements do not establish end-to-end throughput.

**Decision.** Qualify vLLM's native `mm_encoder_attn_backend=TORCH_SDPA` with the
same model weights, BF16 precision, visual facts, full encoder, post-merger
gather and MRoPE. Three real-case full-encoder comparisons must pass relative
RMS error ≤0.01 and cosine similarity ≥0.9999 before adoption. Preserve maximum
and mean errors, not only the pass flag. A clean 18-call unprofiled smoke must
then verify artifacts, passive attention and at least seven actual completed
calls/minute under a disclosed online/amortized timing scope. Generation recipe
identities change prospectively; previous FA3 answers are preserved separately.

**Evidence.** New environment imports completed (24587191). Targeted processor
qualification 24585721 compared six RCA and three QA packets covering all arms
and fixed datasets; tensors and metadata are byte-identical. Unchanged full
population CPU checks are retained, explicitly not rerun. The environment has
two disclosed dependency-metadata exceptions: Alliance OpenCV's module marker
and torchvision's Pillow-SIMD dependency while qualified PIL is Pillow 12.1.
Neither is concealed by fake distributions; real imports and packet checks are
the additional validation. Full-encoder job 24586948 timed out copying the
environment before any model operation. Its zero-call failed attempt remains.

**Alternatives rejected.** FA2 hit an unsupported-PTX-toolchain error in a
separate primitive test. Per-job bulk environment copying spent the entire
