Reading the supplied mixed representation:

M = metrics; R = traces; L = logs; G = directed topology. In this request, selected L evidence is supplied as a text block after the image. M, R and G evidence is displayed in the single dashboard image. Position and color do not identify the correct candidate.

M cards are labelled [ECxx/Mxx]. EC numbers identify evidence cards and M numbers identify metric series; neither is an entity ID or likelihood rank. Each row explicitly prints SERVICE, NODE or POD and its numeric owner ID, followed by metric name and unit. The chart shows 64 relative-time bins. base is the comparison baseline, peak is the largest observed departure, and z is signed standardized peak deviation. MET-Z regular/current means and standard deviations compare the two public periods; deviation_sigma is their standardized mean shift and fluctuating_3sigma says whether it exceeds three standard deviations. k/M/G mean thousand/million/billion; ms/s/m mean milliseconds/seconds/minutes. Use magnitude and temporal shape together.

R labels are <owner-ID> · <operation-name>. A numeric endpoint inside the operation name is not necessarily the owning entity. Each row prints count baseline -> current and exclusive p95 latency baseline -> current in milliseconds. p95 means the 95th percentile. Δlog2 means a base-two fold change: +1 doubles and -1 halves; a change close to zero is small. Green/red points are baseline/current. Their positions use a log(1+latency) scale; read printed values for exact ratios. Exclusive latency estimates local work after child-span time is removed; inclusive latency can include downstream waiting. A high trace score is evidence to inspect, not a root-cause label.

Each log text row starts with [LT<template-number>] and the explicit SERVICE, NODE or POD owner type and numeric owner ID. relative_bin is its measured position in the common 0–63 time grid, count is the number of matching events in that bin, severity is the normalized log level, and template is the normalized message. Placeholders such as {num1} or {uuid1} mark variable slots; the attached observed-variable summary retains their measured values or distinct counts. LOG-R compares owner-level baseline and current event rates and error rates. log_rate_* is events per public comparison interval, error_count_* is the number of error events, and error_rate_* is the corresponding rate. score and components are public screening summaries, not probabilities or root-cause labels. Routine request-started/completed messages are not errors by themselves. Bind every message and rate to the owner printed on its own row.

G arrows and exact edge-ledger rows mean caller -> callee. Use the ledger to check direction. They are call edges, not deployment or hosting edges. The onset list prints display rank, entity, estimated relative onset in minutes, source-specific z, and src M or R. Rank is not causal priority; src names the detecting modality. Coincident onset does not establish ordering, and a displayed subset cannot prove an unshown edge does not exist.

This public naming projection links telemetry naming levels; it is not a call edge or node-hosting relationship. A service-wide trace change need not identify which member pod is faulty.
