
Trace rate comparison: Count is the total number of observed spans in each period; periods can have different lengths. The R header prints baseline/current durations in minutes. Observed spans/min is count divided by that period's minutes. Its ratio is current rate divided by baseline rate: 1× is unchanged, 2× twice the rate, 0.5× half the rate. Compare these rates, not totals alone, when assessing observed traffic changes. These are recorded span rates, not guaranteed true request throughput. Read local latency and other evidence independently; a volume change alone does not identify the root cause.
