Reading the supplied mixed representation:

M = metrics; R = traces; L = logs; G = directed topology. In this request, selected R evidence is supplied as a text block after the image. M, L and G evidence is displayed in the single dashboard image. Position and color do not identify the correct candidate.

M cards are labelled [ECxx/Mxx]. EC numbers identify evidence cards and M numbers identify metric series; neither is an entity ID or likelihood rank. Each row explicitly prints SERVICE, NODE or POD and its numeric owner ID, followed by metric name and unit. The chart shows 64 relative-time bins. base is the comparison baseline, peak is the largest observed departure, and z is signed standardized peak deviation. MET-Z regular/current means and standard deviations compare the two public periods; deviation_sigma is their standardized mean shift and fluctuating_3sigma says whether it exceeds three standard deviations. k/M/G mean thousand/million/billion; ms/s/m mean milliseconds/seconds/minutes. Use magnitude and temporal shape together.

Each trace text row starts with [R<entry-number>] and the explicit SERVICE, NODE or POD owner type and numeric owner ID. operation names the measured endpoint or span operation; numeric text inside an operation name is not necessarily an entity. count_base and count_current compare call counts. exclusive_p95_base_ms and exclusive_p95_current_ms compare local-operation 95th-percentile latency after child-span time is removed. inclusive_p95_current_ms includes child-span time. A large inclusive value with a much smaller exclusive value can therefore indicate downstream waiting. count_delta_log2 and latency_delta_log2 are base-two fold changes: +1 doubles and -1 halves. rank_score is the registered positive TRC-L screening score, evidence to inspect rather than a root-cause label.

L rows give an LT template ID, owner entity, relative bin, event count, severity and normalized message. Placeholders identify variable slots. Log variable summaries give n occurrences, values×frequency, or first/last/min/max/distinct. They are observed data, not diagnosis scores. LOG-R error/event rates compare baseline -> current for that owner; volume_drop and new_errors describe those measured changes. A request-started/completed message is not itself an error. A point on a row timeline locates that template/bin, not the entire case-wide rate comparison.

G arrows and exact edge-ledger rows mean caller -> callee. Use the ledger to check direction. They are call edges, not deployment or hosting edges. The onset list prints display rank, entity, estimated relative onset in minutes, source-specific z, and src M or R. Rank is not causal priority; src names the detecting modality. Coincident onset does not establish ordering, and a displayed subset cannot prove an unshown edge does not exist.

The NAME GROUPS strip in G pairs a 3-digit service alias with its 5-digit pod-name members. This public naming projection links telemetry naming levels; it is not a call edge or node-hosting relationship. A service-wide trace change need not identify which member pod is faulty. Other possible candidates remain in the text candidate list; the strip is not a list of recommended answers.

Each bordered outer card in the image is one evidence bundle. Follow the M/L/G labels rather than assuming fixed screen coordinates. Never infer an entity's type from the number of digits in a card, metric-series, trace-entry or template label.
