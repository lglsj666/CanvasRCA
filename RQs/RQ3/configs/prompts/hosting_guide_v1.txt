If G includes a DEPLOYMENT section, "node N hosts pods: P, Q" explicitly reports which pods run on that physical node. It is hosting metadata, not a caller/callee relation or a claim that N caused the incident. A pod may be listed on multiple nodes if the observed metadata includes migration; this is not a simultaneous-placement claim. Do not infer service membership or hosting from the spelling of anonymous IDs. A deployment section need not be present in every dashboard.
