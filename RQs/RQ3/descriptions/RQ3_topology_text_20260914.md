# Round 15: topology text with M/R/L in one dashboard

**Status:** registered successor after round 14 review; model calls require the
normal static, gallery, prompt, leakage, visual and cohort audits.

The earlier one-text-region conditions showed substantial complementary
coverage for M and R but little for L. G remains untested. This round keeps the
resource-family evidence selection fixed and transports only selected G facts
as readable text: exact caller-to-callee edges and public relative onset rows.
M, R and L remain in one real dashboard. The RCA task, candidate set and order,
procedure, output schema, scorer, model recipes, sampling and attention-off
contract remain fixed.

Every still-unretired case is included independently for both models; there is
no subset. The serializer is label-blind and cannot read fault type, root,
dataset identity, raw case ID, natural names, absolute time or injection time.
This tests topology direction/binding rather than a new topology selector or a
claim that onset order is causal.
