## Decision and next work

Do not promote the all-hosting augmentation. Keep the public relationship
compiler/painter as a selectable capability and retain all negative outcomes.
Evidence additions alone are not enough: top-z selection and individually
auto-scaled metric lanes repeatedly encourage tiny/capacity changes to be read
as severe resource stress. Next develop label-blind diagnostic contrast/metric
scale or entity-aligned bundles, with a separately registered small train
trial. Do not alter this completed batch, re-run mistakes until correct, or
promote a twelve-case score to the full-data targets.
