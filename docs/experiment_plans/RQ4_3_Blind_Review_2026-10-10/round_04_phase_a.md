My independently chosen **research-plan-readiness rubric** is below. Each dimension receives a score from 1 to 10; the overall score is the weighted mean. These are my review criteria, informed by the sources above rather than copied from a venue’s scoring policy.

| Dimension | Weight | What a strong, ready plan must specify |
|---|---:|---|
| **Contribution** | 15% | A precise unresolved question, closest prior approaches, a meaningful anticipated advance, and conclusions that remain useful if the main hypothesis fails. Claims have explicit scope. |
| **Comparison fairness** | 20% | Credible strong baselines; comparable information access, model/task settings, tuning opportunity, and evaluation rules. Differences needed for realistic deployment are disclosed and complemented by controlled comparisons. |
| **Identifiable mechanisms** | 20% | Competing explanations and interventions that distinguish them. Ablations separate selection, representation, model behavior, and computation where relevant. Measurements such as attention are interpreted according to what they identify. |
| **Statistical validity** | 20% | Prespecified estimands, endpoints and contrasts; justified independent sampling units; paired analysis where appropriate; uncertainty and practically meaningful effects; subgroup reporting; selection/multiplicity handling; leakage controls; complete failure denominators. |
| **Full deployment cost** | 15% | Matched quality and cost configurations; preprocessing, encoding, inference and output costs; hardware/software, precision, batching and caching conditions; latency distributions, memory, throughput and failures. Development cost and amortization assumptions are separately visible. |
| **Reproducibility** | 10% | Versioned data/splits, executable configurations, prompts and input construction, model/scorer versions, seeds, per-unit outputs, exclusions and retry accounting, and sufficient artifacts to reconstruct reported comparisons. |

Common score anchors:

- **1–2:** central definitions or design are absent or contradictory.
- **3–4:** substantive design exists, but major threats prevent interpretation.
- **5–6:** plausible study with consequential unresolved design choices.
- **7–8:** coherent, executable protocol with bounded, disclosed limitations.
- **9–10:** unusually complete specification, convincing threat controls, and demonstrated feasibility.

My provisional readiness threshold is **8/10 overall**, with **comparison fairness, mechanism identification, and statistical validity each at least 7**. Unresolved leakage, incompatible primary comparisons, unidentified sampling units, or an unmeasurable central deployment claim block a “ready” recommendation regardless of the average.

Readiness concerns whether the planned study can produce interpretable evidence. It neither requires positive results nor predicts publication acceptance. Acceptance additionally depends on the eventual findings, their importance, demonstrated generality, presentation, and venue judgment.
