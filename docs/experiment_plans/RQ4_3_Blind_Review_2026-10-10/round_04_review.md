**Selection and geometry.** The selectors are specified sufficiently to implement and falsify. The ledger, indivisible-card closure, candidate/modality protection, typed compatible fields, finite-value quantiles, missing signatures, rank normalization, objective weights, geometric budget, greedy tie-breaking, and hash completion are explicit. D and R have different objectives, while J has a fixed combination. Unknown units and singleton records are not converted into invented comparability. C_SOURCE_HASH shares the selection constraints and therefore tests whether the objective adds value over feasible arbitrary selection.

The main selection risk is starvation: mandatory closures may consume most of the available geometry, and exact operation/unit/type matching may leave many singleton groups or a zero distinguishability objective. The existing required reports of protected share, comparable members, singleton counts, objective gain, hash-fill fraction, and actual set changes are the right response. These are CPU feasibility questions. Do not relax keys, borrow private labels, or retune weights after seeing scores merely to force different arms. Such cancellation is an informative result, not a reason to count the condition as successfully evaluated.

PACK has a finite search space in processor coordinates, fixed cards and plots, original bindings, explicit collision rejection, and actual anchor-union accounting. Raising processed fonts to 18 pixels without enlarging cards is a serious feasibility risk given the historical 12–16 pixel observations. The required 10% anchor reduction on at least half the cases in each dataset may be hard to obtain while increasing font size. Nevertheless, the contradiction is empirical feasibility, not an undefined algorithm: the contract says to fail/cancel rather than shrink fonts or silently expand the canvas.

OFFSET tests sensitivity to grid alignment within a changed rendering; it does not isolate every consequence of PACK. BG_FILL holds the successful PACK image and principal allocation fixed and replaces the actual extra observation allocation with foreground-free background locations. It is a much more relevant control than merely renaming an unchanged mask. It tests the value of these retained locations, not the information-free nature of their globally mixed features. Shared support, D=0, insufficient background, and ineffective replacement are explicitly handled.

HORIZON's band formula, native-unit scale, sign semantics, original x coordinates, interpolation crossings, and missing-sample bridging are specified. High-offset series with small variations may waste effective resolution and should remain a prominent qualification example. The plan already includes that boundary case. Since all R arms change minimum font size and can change token allocation, their contrasts against the reading-guide control identify complete rendering policies. I accept the explicit decision not to add a font-matched arm, provided no eventual claim attributes their entire benefit to horizon encoding or packing alone.

**Internal processing and cache semantics.** Static source inspection supports the contract's chosen insertion points. Native GDN computes projections and the directional short convolution, obtains beta and g, repeats Q/K to the value-head count, runs the recurrence, updates the native cache, then applies the gated normalization and output projection. The torch fallback accepts but does not operationally use cu_seqlens. Batch-isolated groups with neutral padded steps therefore have a clear purpose; merely passing packed sequence boundaries would not implement source resets in this installed function.

The proposed local branch reuses those post-convolution vectors, reverses Q/K/V/g/beta together, starts each auxiliary state at zero, restores positions, and mixes before the existing gated norm. The 0.75 native + 0.25 bidirectional-local mixture is a coherent intervention. It does not create a trained bidirectional model and does not undo the native directional convolution. Group locality is geometric provenance; vision features already contain global mixing. The plan correctly avoids claiming independent source-information channels or exact permutation invariance.

Cache consistency is appropriately defined against the modified prefill: each layer keeps its native state computed from that layer's actual input, later layers can acquire altered caches because their inputs changed, and auxiliary states are discarded. Requiring every later cache to equal U would be the wrong test. Conversely, a valid JSON answer cannot verify this semantics. The registered lambda-zero, tiny recurrence, padding, scatter, group-renaming, and cached-decode checks are necessary before qualification.

SOFT_IMAGE explicitly specifies allowed image-query keys, preserves text-query causality, and combines two normalized attention outputs before the single existing sigmoid gate and o_proj. This agrees with the inspected attention class's ordering. It is not the original SoFA mask equation and is properly labeled as an adaptation. P_DUAL has a specified sequential composition. The fixed coefficient/layers avoid a hidden tuning budget but remain unproven engineering choices.

FORWARD, SHAM, and GLOBAL respectively test reversal, meaningful grouping, and group scope. FORWARD has less compute, so an equal-FLOP interpretation would be invalid. SHAM's deterministic mapping and co-group-pair change audit prevent an almost-identical grouping from masquerading as a strong control. The order intervention keeps spatial coordinates with tokens while changing serial slots; it acts through convolution, recurrence, and downstream processing together. Its attribution should remain that broad even if LOCAL reduces flips.

The torch fallback and extra scans may be slow. Bucket batching prevents a new Python loop per token, but it does not prove low overhead, and short padded groups can be inefficient. These concerns make cost measurement essential; they do not require changing the frozen operator before a bounded correctness/coverage assessment.

**Fair comparisons, QA, and inference.** GenerationIdentityV1 and HistoricalCompatibilityCertificate are load-bearing. Identical checkpoint names or agreement on 12/60 answers cannot certify generation equivalence over 220 cases. If compatibility is not demonstrated, historical-U primary, optimization, interaction, and U-order tests lose eligibility; p=1 is an accounting placeholder, not evidence of no effect. The contract explicitly handles this. Contemporary attribution contrasts can still answer narrower questions, and a later genuinely unexposed same-stack X/U/T cohort can support its own comparison. There is no scientific basis to spend 220 replacement baseline calls automatically merely to preserve the original table.

The factorial contrasts are well defined for the specified S/J, R/PACK, and P/LOCAL factors, with X_SRP chosen in advance. Historical stack offsets do not cancel automatically in these contrasts. Rendering includes its guide, so interaction claims concern the registered policies rather than a pure font-free representation effect. The exact-reference/fallback table handles the particularly important case of an unchanged image with changed reading instructions.

QA addresses historical failures by asking explicit source-bound values, entity/unit/phase binding, graphical magnitude comparison, and observed directed relations. Fixed pool targets, source paths, display precision, unavailable responses, structural NA, and per-case aggregation are specified. The marked QA images are a separate measurement input; the contract correctly recomputes masks and source mappings rather than assuming RCA geometry is unchanged. The endpoint combines target availability and correct reading. Common-visible accuracy is a useful descriptive decomposition, but cannot replace that fixed endpoint when a selector removes difficult targets. Six questions do not create six independent incidents. QA–RCA correlation does not establish mediation, and six questions cannot certify complete trend reasoning.

The same 60 cases for U/P order-versus-repeat comparisons are necessary to distinguish intervention flips from repeat noise. The 12-case R/X repeats are only coarse checks. Scoring order-versus-base RR as well as flips avoids equating any changed answer with deterioration.

**Statistical validity and total cost.** The 220 development cases are exposed. The primary sampling structure is the 193 audited event groups, and the plan separates case-weighted, group-weighted, and dataset-macro estimands. This is preferable to treating correlated incident windows, questions, or patches as independent samples. Pratt's signed-rank position null requires its symmetry interpretation; it is not a general test of case-weighted mean MRR. The contract states this explicitly. I do not request confidence intervals contrary to the user's scope. The planned paired effects, tests, subgroup results, repeat measurements, failure bounds, and candid power limitations still provide useful evidence.

The planning sensitivity calculation is not proof of power for X or proof of CPU feasibility. Its reported roughly 20–54% detection rates for a +.03 group-mean effect under two modeled scenarios warn that a non-significant result could remain inconclusive. The .03 case-mean adoption target is another estimand. The separate Holm families are reasonable for separate questions if all registered comparisons are reported; they do not imply study-wide 5% familywise error. A new label cannot move an inconvenient test into a more favorable family.

The development cost design now measures a continuous case-cold path with model loading separately recorded, using the same HF stack, BF16, concurrency, cache and attention settings. It includes compilation, rendering, processing, full vision encoding, selection/gather, prefill, decoding, saving, failures, and retries. This directly addresses the historical gap between token savings and deployment latency. It supports a single-request deployment claim; broad throughput or production-load claims would need different evidence and are not presently required.

Twelve cases, only four per dataset, cannot precisely establish a 5% population latency advantage or stable p95. The proposal calls this an engineering gate and measures the full confirmation cohort if that stage becomes eligible. That limitation is acceptable at this stage. Report actual means and per-case ratios, keep the registered ratio-of-means gate, and avoid treating a noisy gate pass as general equivalence. Optional attention collection must remain disabled for matched timing and default off if its smoke check fails. No inference about zero overhead follows from a single on/off pair.

The conditional confirmation module has a real stopping rule: fixed X, complete development batch, strong-text and visual progress, cost feasibility, exposure audit, sufficient independent groups, and no unresolved unknowns. Its existence is uncertain until the audit. A minimum of 30 groups per dataset is a feasibility floor, not a power guarantee. The separate confirmation success definition prevents passing a development entry gate from becoming a final success claim. Full-cohort current-stack cost collection adds no timing-only calls.

**Must-fix versus already registered gates.** I identify **no new design amendment required before bounded CPU qualification**. The following are mandatory release conditions already in the frozen design, not extra arms or new approval requirements introduced by this review:

1. Establish historical inferential eligibility or explicitly activate D2's restricted analysis before the first model call. Do not present an unverified stack as equivalent.
2. Establish actual selection freedom, distinctness, readable/legal PACK or HORIZON geometry, effective BG_FILL/OFFSET interventions, and sufficient source-local/SHAM changes on the registered denominators. Cancel failed candidates and their dependent combinations under the existing rules.
3. Qualify the modified prefill and cache algorithm and the independent QA scoring/input path. Static agreement with an insertion point does not establish numerical or runtime correctness.
4. Complete the bounded registered smoke and applicable cost checks before any formal inference, then preserve exact identities, complete accounting, and cold-path timing during the batch. Audit independent-cohort exposure before claiming confirmation eligibility.

None was performed by this reviewer. These conditions distinguish “sensible next qualification” from “ready for unrestricted execution.”

Optional improvements require no new diagnostic calls: put the historical compatibility outcome first in the eventual results table; make selector zero-objective/hash-fill and rendering failure reasons prominent; stratify descriptive horizon QA errors by high-offset/small-variation cases; report P's group length/padding and auxiliary-time distributions beside retained-token counts. These are clearer uses of already specified artifacts, not requests for additional experiments. A font-only arm, coefficient sweep, fresh full historical baselines, another multi-stage design, or a second model is not required for this bounded proposal.

**Budget and additional calls.** The specified ledger is internally coherent: 4,400 main positions + 540 QA + 120 order + 144 repeats + 12 current T timing = 5,216; adding the conditional 1,080 confirmation reserve, 18 smoke positions, and 234 recovery reserve gives 6,548, leaving 1,452 unallocated beneath 8,000. Exact references, cancellation, and inapplicability can reduce actual generation; the ceiling is not a spending target. Historical U/T/hybrid positions are references, not permission to repeat them.

The seven controls have identifiable questions rather than serving as nominal variants. The background and global controls are particularly useful for separating observation-allocation value from token count and source grouping from generic extra mixing. Matching U/P repeats to the same 60 cases and the 12 current T timing calls are justified by the corresponding noise and cost comparisons. **I request zero additional calls beyond the frozen allocation.** Conditional confirmation is necessary only if making a supported independent performance/cost claim; if it cannot run or fails, publish the narrower development/diagnostic evidence honestly rather than filling the remaining budget.

**Publication readiness is separate.** This is not yet publication-ready evidence for outperforming strong text RCA at comparable deployment cost. No proposed method has demonstrated that result, the development pool is exposed, historical compatibility is pending, and the independent cohort may not exist. A successful outcome could support a useful contribution, but the ingredient list alone would not establish one: selectors and horizon encoding are established ideas, bidirectional attention interpolation has prior art, and the proposed local GDN adaptation needs measured value and faithful mechanistic limits. A negative study could still be informative if it establishes where apparent coverage, reading accuracy, and token savings fail to translate into reliable diagnosis. Venue acceptance cannot be inferred from this readiness score.

My recommendation is to retain the frozen design and proceed only to its bounded qualification when authorized. Another review round is not needed merely because those empirical gates have not run; it would be warranted if qualification exposes a consequential ambiguity, forces an operator/claim change, or removes comparisons needed for the intended conclusion.
