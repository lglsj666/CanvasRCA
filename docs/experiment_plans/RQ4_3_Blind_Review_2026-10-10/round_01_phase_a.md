**Fixed rubric for Phase B**

| Dimension | Weight | What a ready plan must specify |
|---|---:|---|
| Research question and novelty | 15% | A falsifiable claim; closest prior methods; the precise new contribution; useful knowledge from a negative result. |
| Baselines and comparability | 20% | Strong relevant baselines and simple controls; matched information access, budgets, tuning opportunity, and evaluation units; justified exclusions. |
| Attribution and mechanism | 20% | Interventions that separate component effects and plausible confounds; mechanism claims bounded by what those interventions identify. Attractive visualizations or correlations alone are insufficient. |
| Statistical validity | 20% | Primary outcome and meaningful effect size; independent sampling unit; paired/group-aware uncertainty; multiplicity and selection handling; complete failure denominator; protected confirmation protocol. |
| Deployment cost and feasibility | 15% | Actual measurement boundaries, hardware/software, preprocessing and added instrumentation overhead, memory, latency/throughput, and accuracy–cost tradeoffs on the same configuration. |
| Reproducibility and decision rules | 10% | Executable protocol, provenance, exclusion/failure rules, bounded resources, and predefined decisions for positive, null, or contradictory outcomes. |

I will grade each dimension **0–4**: absent, seriously deficient, partially specified, adequate, strong. The readiness score is **1 + 9 × weighted mean / 4**, reported to one decimal with substantive blockers stated separately.

Interpretation: **1–3** concept requiring redesign; **4–5** major protocol repair; **6–7** credible plan with material gaps; **8–9** strong and executable; **10** exceptionally complete. This measures **research-plan readiness**, not predicted paper acceptance, guaranteed improvement, or enthusiasm for the proposed method.
