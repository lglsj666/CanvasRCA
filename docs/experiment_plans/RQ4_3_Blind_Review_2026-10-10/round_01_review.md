# RQ4.3 independent review — round 1

**Research-plan readiness: 6.7/10. CPU-readiness verdict: conditional, not yet ready for integrated qualification against a frozen specification.** The proposal is worth developing. It has a credible starting point, a useful comparison matrix, explicit limits on attribution, and a workable call ceiling. It still leaves several consequential algorithm, measurement, and decision rules to be invented during implementation. Resolve those before treating a CPU pass as qualification of the registered experiment. Small, label-blind feasibility work can follow the relevant specification fixes; it need not wait for an independent confirmation cohort or proof of positive model performance.

This is a readiness judgment, not a paper-acceptance score or a prediction that the method will succeed. I received no other review or requested target score. I did not inspect other round material. No experiment, renderer, model, test, or server was executed, and no plan, code, or Codex authority was edited. This review and the verbatim Phase A record are the only deliverables written.

## 2. What the historical evidence actually supports

Starting from RQ4.1 U is sensible: its MRR is 0.3517, compared with CVC 0.3332, full visual 0.2209, and T_FULL 0.4846. RQ4.2's strongest new visual arm is still below U. A larger or more sophisticated visual path has not reliably helped. The measured 86%–90% anchor occupancy makes PATCH_PACK a concrete engineering hypothesis. The nearly saturated dependency-support proxy and extremely similar CVC/rewired masks explain why another small coverage-weight adjustment would be weakly motivated. [RQ4.1 §§4,8; RQ4.2 §§4,6.]

There is nevertheless a large gap to the text objective: approximately 0.133 MRR from U to T_FULL. A +0.03 improvement over U would be useful development progress and would leave most of that gap. The plan correctly says so. Neither fewer invalid IDs nor higher auxiliary QA alone is a plausible complete explanation of the historical gap. RQ4.1's invalid-output analysis and RQ4.2's small invalid-output count constrain that story. [RQ4.1 §7; RQ4.2 §11.]

RQ3.16 MARGINAL_FIVE at 0.5054 is a hybrid reference, not a pure-visual method to inherit. Its small difference from MARKLESS, dataset Top-5 declines, and different runtime make it unsuitable as evidence that visual marks already supplied a large causal benefit. RQ3.21 further shows that reversible representation does not ensure successful model reading. RQ3.20 shows how nominal evidence budgets and schema correctness can coexist with ineffective evidence access; its direct-answer QA cards are a caution about mechanism measurements, not a reason to reopen two-stage RCA. [RQ3.16 §§4–6,8; RQ3.21 §§7–9; RQ3.20 §§6–10.]

RQ4.2's SEM-minus-GEOM difference is approximately −0.0004 under case averaging and +0.007 under event averaging. That is a concrete warning to define the estimand before interpreting a significance test. Its repaired QA covered two Trace p95 cards and a directed relation, rather than all diagnostic modalities. [RQ4.2 §§5,8.]

## 3. Novelty and whether the comparisons answer the claims

The defensible contribution is a measured, provenance-aware adaptation to this frozen VLM and telemetry task: selecting complete comparable sources, compiling evidence for the actual token grid, and testing source-local language-side processing at a fixed visual budget. Facility-location coverage, two-band horizon graphs, bidirectionality, and equal-weight combinations are established ingredients. The plan appropriately avoids claiming otherwise.

S_DISTINGUISH and S_REPRESENT have distinct objectives. The former rewards retaining both sides of a public comparison; the latter covers the distribution of records. That is enough to retain both proposed slots pending actual-set checks. S_JOINT is a combination, not an independent theoretical invention. Against C_SOURCE_HASH, these test the choice of evidence under shared constraints; against U, they test the total consequence of omission and allocation. Neither contrast identifies an intrinsic advantage of visual representation over text. The plan's stated boundaries are appropriate. Report actual retained package cost/count and coverage as well as the shared upper budget: differently sized packages can cause different budget utilization.

PATCH_PACK is the strongest task-specific engineering hypothesis. R versus C_PACK_BG_FILL can test whether allocating released slots to observation positions helps, on the same encoded image. R versus OFFSET tests sensitivity to alignment with substantial feature changes; it cannot by itself isolate token-count mediation. The added background control is valuable, and its contextualized-background limitation is correctly acknowledged. A positive packing result can still be useful without a clean attention explanation.

HORIZON is a distinct encoding trial and deserves its slot if geometry and QA are meaningful. I would not replace it merely because older heatmaps/atlases failed. However, two bands over a zero-based absolute range may make small changes on a large positive baseline visually negligible. That is a falsifiable risk, not demonstrated infeasibility. Preserve original exact fields and inspect such examples; do not rescue the arm after outcomes by switching to unregistered residuals or adaptive band counts.

P_LOCAL_BI targets the language backbone, unlike RQ4.2's vision-encoder intervention. The local-forward, sham and global-bidirectional controls distinguish useful alternatives. Correct locality versus a single long group changes reset boundaries and available context jointly; the resulting claim should remain about this organization of computation. P_SOFT_IMAGE is a useful alternative route and P_DUAL a fixed combination. SparseVLM-style pruning and SoFA are relevant nearest work, but adding faithful external implementations is unnecessary to answer these narrow questions when they change architectural assumptions or budgets. [SparseVLM](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.04417v3), [SoFA](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.13792).

The fixed 2×2×2 design is stronger than adding the best observed single-arm gains. Its factors are operational bundles: R includes its reading guide and changed visual features; S changes facts; P changes computation. The factorial effects can identify nonadditivity of those bundles on the registered outcome scale. They cannot establish three independent biological-style mechanisms. The four contrasts are appropriate, but their multiplicity rule remains unstated.

I find no substantive reason now to remove one of the three requested candidate positions in each direction. Actual duplication or failed qualification can justify cancellation under the existing gates. Do not force distinctions through post hoc weight changes.

## 4. Must fix before integrated CPU qualification

These are missing specifications that can be fixed now. They do not require observing new RCA scores.

**M1 — Make the S selectors executable without scientific choices left to the implementer.** Plan §4.1 explicitly defers precise fields/formulas. Freeze the quantile vector, rank normalization and ties, distance/similarity, weights, incomparable-record treatment, zero upper-bound convention, and source-cost aggregation when supports overlap. State whether protected coverage means every entity×available-modality pair or separate entity and modality cover constraints. Give deterministic pseudocode for single/pair proposals, closure, budget accounting, zero-gain fill, and ties. Define the exact historical methods and manifests used for the duplication screen. A compact schema plus a few hand-computable synthetic examples suffices; no new candidate or percentage sweep is needed.

**M2 — Define the finite PATCH_PACK/HORIZON geometry problem and resolve its readability constraint.** Plan §4.2 supplies a lexicographic objective, not its admissible layout moves or search/stopping rule. Specify allowed block positions, wrapping, plot-area displacement, bounds and conflicts, actual processed foreground support, and whether global candidate strings can move. Freeze the processor transform used in the objective. U had processed text around 12–16 pixels; requiring at least 18 while preserving complete strings within old cards may require enlargement and may reduce packing freedom. This is a feasibility question to expose before full rendering, not a contradiction already proved. Clarify whether 18 pixels is a hard qualification threshold, which text it covers, and how it is measured. For HORIZON freeze the exact nice-bandwidth calculation, positive/negative folding, boundaries, interpolation and legend mapping. Define processed-coordinate measurement, rather than relying on nominal font size. Inspect long labels, high offsets with small changes, zero/negative values and missing-sample connections under the existing renderer rules.

**M3 — Close the P and order-intervention operator contract.** The main recurrence mixing equation is clear. The executable contract still needs the installed-kernel entry point/version, tensor shapes and normalizations, reverse gate indexing, variable-length packing, scatter identity, and exact native/local state ownership. For SOFT_IMAGE explicitly define the allowed key set for every query type, including preceding text and image delimiters; preserve causal text behavior. For the order test freeze noncontiguous-source packing, fixed global/shared slots and the complete permutation construction. Cache correctness should be checked against a reference implementing this modified visual-prefill algorithm on the same fixed prefix, not against an ordinary all-causal forward that represents a different algorithm. At later layers, native text processing legitimately receives changed preceding visual states; do not require its numerical equality to U. These specifications allow meaningful tiny reference checks and kernel qualification without training or a second diagnosis.

**M4 — Write one inheritance/identity and fallback table.** Bind U and T_FULL to exact historical response identities, cases, candidate dictionaries, scorer version, messages, processor, retained indices/MRoPE, checkpoint identity, precision, backend, seed policy and ranking-prefix decoder. RQ4.1 used a per-case recipe; RQ4.2 changed prompts/layout and used seed 42. “Existing local model/original decoding” must not accidentally inherit the latter while comparing to the former. Record intentional deltas by arm. For composite/control inapplicability, name the actual equivalent request: for example, a background replacement with D=0 is ordinarily identical to R, not automatically U; an untriggered P inside SRP can be identical to SR. Reference only after input **and computation** equality. Unknown structures remain qualification failures. This is bookkeeping from preserved artifacts, not a request to regenerate 660 responses or rehash the entire historical artifact graph on every restart.

A failed gate should stop only its dependants, as the plan already says. Exact kernel speed and whether at least half the cases admit useful packing are empirical qualification results, not things the prose must guarantee. Conversely, a legal JSON response or unchanged token count cannot stand in for those checks.

## 5. Must fix before new model calls or an adoption claim

**M5 — Freeze a QA instrument that actually measures the changed representations.** The inherited repaired interface is useful, but simply reusing its Trace/relation targets would leave the HORIZON intervention largely unmeasured. Within the existing six-question ceiling and 540 calls, preregister a label-blind target mix including metric values/trends or two-phase comparisons, identity/unit binding and relevant relations. Freeze eligibility, ties, numerical tolerance, unit conversion and denominator rules. Avoid ambiguity from multi-service traces; do not provide answer cards. If target markers are added, freeze their renderer provenance and verify each QA arm's actual token mask/source groups. The QA input is then a marked diagnostic measurement, not byte-identical to RCA. Keep fixed-pool coverage, end-to-end answering and common-visible accuracy separate, as already proposed. Positive ΔQA with null ΔMRR does not establish independence or complete mediation failure.

**M6 — Finish the statistical protocol and power assessment.** Define the primary effect estimand and event-group aggregation explicitly. The historical 220 cases correspond to 193 groups, with AIOPS-2022 particularly clustered (33 groups for 60 cases). Equal event weighting, pooled case MRR and equal-dataset macro MRR answer different questions. Pratt signed-rank tests do not generally test the mean MRR difference under arbitrary asymmetric effects; state their null/assumptions and bound the conclusion accordingly. Report the registered effect alongside case/group and dataset directions; preserve the project's no-CI rule rather than silently introducing intervals. Set sidedness, alpha, zero-variance handling, the four-interaction correction, and the exact mechanism hypotheses/statistics and correction family. Do not let an open-ended QA family expand after seeing results. Assess power/minimum detectable effects with historical paired variability and realistic effect patterns before spending on the full matrix. The older conservative RQ4.1 analysis showed severe power limitations under its own test; that numerical power is not automatically transferable to the new Pratt protocol. More arms on the same groups do not solve low independent sample size.

The 12-case repeat block is adequate for detecting gross instability, not for precise claims that 60-case order effects exceed stochastic noise. Either restrict that claim explicitly or preallocate a justified increase from the reserve. Do not demand wholesale repeated baselines merely because small replication is imperfect.

**M7 — Make deployment cost an actual decision rule and account for its measurements.** Plan §8.4 defines a token ratio but says only to strive for unchanged total latency. Freeze the exact practical latency/memory criterion, baseline denominator, aggregation, cold versus warm boundaries, concurrency and instrumentation policy. U's historical online time was about 20.83 seconds versus T_FULL 22.13; those omit enough preparation that a token advantage cannot certify a full deployment advantage. RQ4.2 likewise lacks several isolated stage measurements. Time source preparation/selection, renderer, processor, complete visual encoding, gather, prefill, generation and saving consistently; reset memory measurement per arm/request as appropriate. Use current-stack paired timing for U, T_FULL and X on a small registered set. The present repeat list contains no T_FULL timing calls, so specify and count any needed new T requests in the reserve. Reuse historical ranking outcomes for the 220-case accuracy comparison. A small timing study is a justified exception to avoiding wasteful baseline reruns, not a new full accuracy sweep. Publish accuracy and measured cost for the same configuration.

**M8 — Decide how much confirmation this stage promises.** The plan honestly calls these 220 cases exposed development data and leaves new-case validation unallocated. That is acceptable for a development study and does not block CPU feasibility. It cannot support a final independent-generalization claim. Before accessing new-case outcomes, freeze eligible event groups, exposure audit, candidate X, U/T comparisons, call ledger and stopping rules. If no defensible cohort exists within the ceiling, say that confirmation is deferred and limit the resulting claim. Do not substitute whichever of the nine arms wins after development for the preselected X without a separately frozen selection/confirmation protocol.

## 6. Budget, runtime and feasibility judgment

The arithmetic is correct: 4,400 main positions + 540 QA + 120 order + 48 repeats = 5,108 formal positions; 18 smoke + 234 interruption allowance yields 5,360 explicitly allocated, leaving 2,640 under 8,000. Historical references cost no new calls. These are upper allocations, not an obligation to execute identical or cancelled requests. Infrastructure retries and new timing calls must enter the same total; incorrect rankings are never a retry reason.

At a rough 20–25 seconds per serial request, 5,108 calls alone imply about 28–35.5 hours, before compilation, heavier operators or long tails. This is a scale estimate from historical timing, not a runtime forecast: QA and output lengths differ. The formal schedule should therefore include a measured upper-bound planning estimate after qualification. The 18-call/600-second smoke cap is tight enough that startup and saving could matter. Preflight its timing from existing logs and specify fail/stop behavior; do not silently reset or subdivide it. The plan already acknowledges incomplete arm×dataset smoke coverage, which should remain explicit.

The model configuration supports the proposed layer locations. Nothing read proves that the extra local scans are impossible. Variable-length efficient kernels, attention mask semantics and cache correctness still require implementation evidence. A training-free change may degrade the frozen model even with numerically correct code. Likewise, preserving full image facts does not guarantee that the retained tokens encode them well. These are genuine research uncertainties, not current hard contradictions or reasons to prescribe training.

## 7. Priorities and what not to add

First close M1–M4 and register finite, cheap feasibility checks. Before model calls close M5–M7. Treat M8 as mandatory for claims beyond development, rather than as a reason to delay every CPU check. Preserve the nine proposed optimization positions, seven useful controls and fixed factorial; cancel only on the documented feasibility/duplication gates. The fixes chiefly require precise tables/formulas and measurement registration, not a larger arm matrix.

Optional improvements are a small human source-audit template for explanations and a compact input-difference report linking old failures to new public-field coverage. Attention plots, additional models and wider datasets may help a later paper, but should not delay the immediate specification work. The present one-model study needs honest scope, not an automatic multi-model expansion.

Do **not** add a multi-stage evidence selector, textual diagnostic safety net, SFT/RL, output repair/whitelisting, a layer/λ/pruning-rate sweep, a 3×3×3 combination search, another visualization family merely to fill budget, or a 220-case rerun of compatible baselines. Do not tune on new confirmation outcomes or weaken failed gates after scores. Do not claim facility location, horizon graphs or generic bidirectionality as inventions. Do not make attention collection a prerequisite for establishing a deployment improvement.
