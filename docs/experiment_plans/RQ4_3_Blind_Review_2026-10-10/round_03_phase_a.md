**Frozen rubric for subsequent proposal review**

Each dimension receives a **1–10 readiness score**; the weighted mean is the overall research-plan readiness score. The criteria below are my synthesis, not claims that the cited reviewers prescribed this exact rubric.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence required for a high score |
|---|---:|---|
| Novelty and claim precision | 15% | A falsifiable contribution relative to the closest methods. Distinguish a new mechanism, a new empirical finding, and an application adaptation. Scope claims to the conditions tested. |
| Fair baselines | 20% | Strong relevant systems plus simple controls; matched evidence availability, candidates, model/backbone, decoding, tuning opportunity and evaluation definitions. Explain necessary departures. Include useful quality–cost comparisons. |
| Attribution and visual reliance | 20% | Controls isolate evidence selection, representation, model intervention and extra computation. Test whether image content matters. Use interventions to support mechanism claims; attention patterns alone are insufficient. Keep oracle analyses separate from deployable methods. |
| Statistics and selection integrity | 15% | Predeclared primary contrasts, paired case-level analysis, uncertainty intervals, event/source clustering where applicable, dataset breakdowns, and a defensible multiplicity policy. Separate development selection from final testing; preserve failures in denominators. |
| Actual deployment cost | 15% | Measured full-pipeline latency, throughput and peak memory on named hardware/software, with batching and input/output lengths specified. Include preprocessing, rendering/encoding, fusion, retries and instrumentation. Distinguish measured quantities from FLOPs and analytical estimates. |
| Feasibility and reproducibility | 10% | Exact implementable intervention, supported kernels/model interfaces, resource and call budgets, compatibility checks, artifact provenance, and bounded qualification gates. An unimplemented hardware assumption remains a material risk. |
| Scope and failure analysis | 5% | Tests include dense labels, binding errors, temporal relationships and heterogeneous faults where relevant. Separate extraction/representation failure from reasoning failure and quantify where conclusions do not transfer. |

Readiness anchors: **1–3** has fundamental validity or feasibility gaps; **4–5** needs major redesign; **6–7** is executable with material gaps; **8–9** is well specified with bounded residual risks; **10** requires unusually complete planning and qualification evidence.

A missing comparison necessary to identify the central effect caps readiness at **5**; unresolved test leakage caps it at **3**; an unsupported central implementation assumption caps it at **4**. These caps apply only when the issue threatens the principal claim.

I will score **research-plan readiness separately from paper acceptance**. An excellent plan cannot establish acceptance without results, demonstrated significance, and a defensible contribution. No proposal score or acceptance recommendation is assigned in Phase A.
